# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS (user's choice). Motion via GSAP + Lenis from CDN. No build step, so the site can be hosted anywhere and re-skinned per client.

## Users

Primary: women and men, roughly 28 to 55, in a large Brazilian city, with disposable income, researching a premium aesthetic procedure on their phone (often from Instagram or a Google search like "harmonização facial em São Paulo"). They compare clinics on trust: who performs the procedure, how natural the results look, hygiene and safety, and how easy it is to book. Their job: decide whether this clinic is safe and good enough to message on WhatsApp for an evaluation.

Secondary (the real buyer of this demo): the owner of an aesthetic clinic, shown this site by a freelance web designer as a sales demo. It must make them think "I want my clinic to look like this".

## Product Purpose

A demonstration website for a fictional premium aesthetic clinic, used by a freelance designer to sell websites to real clinics. Success: a visitor understands within seconds what the clinic does and why it is trustworthy, and taps to book an evaluation via WhatsApp. For the buyer, success is a site that feels clearly more premium than the Instagram-only or template sites most clinics have.

## Positioning

Fictional clinic: **Seiva — Estética Avançada**, São Paulo (Jardins). Premium, high ticket. Positioned on natural results ("a sua melhor versão, não outra pessoa"), medical responsibility (procedures by registered health professionals), and an evaluation-first process. All of this is demo content to be swapped per client.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive on mobile first, from Instagram bio links, Google Business Profile and Google Ads. Booking happens over WhatsApp; the clinic replies by hand. Evaluations are in person.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services to cover: facial injectables (toxina botulínica, preenchimento com ácido hialurônico, harmonização facial, bioestimuladores de colágeno), skin (limpeza de pele, peeling, microagulhamento, laser para manchas), body (criolipólise, drenagem linfática, tratamento de flacidez e celulite), laser hair removal (depilação a laser por áreas).
- Primary action: WhatsApp booking for an evaluation. A placeholder number is used.
- Brazilian advertising rules for health (CFM / CFO / Anvisa guidance) discourage before/after photos used as promises and price promotions for medical procedures; the site avoids guaranteed-result claims and shows prices only as "a partir de" where appropriate, or not at all.
- Language: Brazilian Portuguese.

## Brand Commitments

Visual look pinned by the user with reference images of premium aesthetic-clinic sites (nude/peach palette, serif display, glowing-skin portraits, torn-paper hero reveal); a first green/yellow modernist direction was rejected. None from a real client. Name, address, phone, team names, and registration numbers are fictional placeholders, to be replaced per client.

## Evidence on Hand

No real testimonials, results, team, awards, or press. Any testimonials or numbers on the demo are illustrative and must be clearly replaceable; no invented awards, press logos, or medical claims.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before desire: safety, who performs, and process are as visible as beauty.
2. Natural results over transformation; never promise outcomes.
3. One action everywhere: book an evaluation on WhatsApp.
4. Mobile is the primary screen.
5. Easy to re-skin: content and colours are centralised so the designer can adapt it per client quickly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, reduced-motion support, readable on low-end Android phones.
