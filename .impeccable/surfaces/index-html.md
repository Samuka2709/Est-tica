# Surface brief: index.html (Seiva landing)

Scope: single-page landing, visitor mode Persuade. Audience: premium aesthetic-clinic prospects on mobile, plus the clinic owner seeing a sales demo. Action: book an evaluation on WhatsApp (prefilled message). Proof: process, who performs, transparency, tools (comparador, calendário do laser), no fabricated testimonials. Constraints: CFM 2.336/2023 (no promised results, no brand names, no superiority adjectives), PT-BR, static HTML.

History: a first direction (Casa Modernista Paulistana, seed:0c696900c8e0a8d7) was rejected ("não tô gostando muito do design, e desse verde e amarelo"). The user pinned the look with reference images of aesthetic-clinic sites and singled out one hero: "Gostei absurdamente dessa hero, faça de tudo para colocar uma assim no site", later "Se necessário, pegue a referência, tire o texto, ajuste, e coloque como nossa hero" and, for the torn edge, "agr faça os negocios assim" (rough, flaky paper). The third-party reference image itself was not reused; an original image was generated instead. The user approved the result: "simplismente lindo".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category's own premium language (nude, serif, glowing skin) executed at full craft, with the face revealed through torn peach paper as the one memorable move; differentiation lives in honest tools and transparency, not in a foreign aesthetic.

OWN-WORLD: Peach paper (#EAB494, matched to the hero image) for the first viewport and the close; porcelain (#F5EEE8) and sand (#ECDDD1) grounds; taupe (#5A4335 / #8A6A57) titles with italic accents in section heads; cocoa (#231B17) for the primary button and dark bands. Playfair Display for display, Jost for text and tracked uppercase buttons. Arch-topped photos, thin line icons.

STORY: The visitor sees a calm, premium clinic, understands evaluation-first and natural results, explores treatments with honest numbers, plans laser by season, and books an evaluation on WhatsApp with a ready message.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed generated image: flat peach paper on the left, an extreme close-up face on the right revealed through a rough, flaky vertical tear. Over the paper, left: roman Playfair headline in two lines "Sua pele, / na sua melhor luz", one promise line, cocoa "Agendar avaliação" + outlined "Ver tratamentos", three line-icon trust items, a row of small social/contact icons. Header: logo left, nav right after it over the flat paper, black "Agendar" at the far right. Mobile: a face crop of the same image on top, text and buttons below on the same peach, CTA inside the first screen.

FORM: pinned by the user's reference images. seed: none (pinned) — "Gostei absurdamente dessa hero, faça de tudo para colocar uma assim no site"; "Se necessário, pegue a referência, tire o texto, ajuste, e coloque como nossa hero". Signature motion: the hero image wipes in from the right on load while the title rises line by line; every photo then reveals as a curtain rising from the bottom (arches keep their rounded top) with a slight zoom-out, the dark photo band zooms as it passes, and section titles rise from behind a line. No other text entrances.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
